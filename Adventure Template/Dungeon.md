# Encounters

---
## Random Encounters
For every 1 hour the PCs spend in the dungeon, roll a d6. On a 1, a random encounter happens. Roll on the random encounter table for the zone they're in to determine what they encounter. Whenever the PCs spend a dungeon turn making loud noises, increase the number required for an encounter to happen on a d6 roll by 1. This number resets if the PCs leave the dungeon and take a long rest, or if 8 hours go by without the number increasing.

## Trap Name
*Nuisance/Deadly Trap (Levels A-B) (X XP)*

***Description.*** Describe the environment around the trap, and possibly bait to draw the PCs into the trap or a red herring so they don't notice it too easily.

***Trigger.*** What sets the trap off?

***Duration.*** How long does the trap last?

***Effect.*** How does the trap work and what happens when it goes off?

***Save.*** Ability Saving Throw, DC X
- *Success.* Result
- *Failure.* Result

***Detect.*** How can the PCs detect the trap before it's triggered?

***Avoid.*** What is a way to get past the trap without triggering it?

***Disarm.*** How can the trap be disabled / destroyed so it doesn't function anymore?

***Escape.*** How can a creature leave the trap if they've been caught in it?

## Water
***Finding a Water Source.*** Search action in the environment, Wisdom (Survival) DC 10 ability check.
- *Success.* A water source is successfully found after d6 hours. Roll a d12 using the table below to determine the type of water source found. Then, whenever a creature collects water from that water source, roll a d100 and use the corresponding chance of poison for that water source to determine if the water they collected is poison.
- *Failure.* After 6 hours of searching, no water source is found.

| d12 | Water Source | Chance of Poison (d100) |
|-|-|-|
| 1-3 | Pond | 30 % |
| 4-7 | Creek | 10 % |
| 8-11 | Stream | 5 % |
| 12 | Creek / Stream Waterfall | 2 % |

***Collecting Rain Water.*** Different equipment can be used to collect rainwater. The amount of rainwater they can collect in a certain amount of time is determined by the level of rainfall occurring (either moderate or heavy) and a die roll based on the table below. Equipment can only be used to collect rainwater if it is made of a watertight material, otherwise the water leaks out of the bottom. If rainwater is being collected with a piece of equipment not listed on this table, you can use a similar piece of equipment on the table to determine how much rainwater it collects.

| Equipment | Moderate Rainfall | Heavy Rainfall |
|-|-|-|
| Backpack (Watertight), Bucket, Iron Pot, Jug | 1 gallon per 1d12 hours | 2d6 gallons per hour |
| Barrel | 1 gallon per 1d6 hours | 2d12 gallons per hour |
| Chest | 1d3 gallons per hour | 1d10 x 3 gallons per hour |
| Case, Flask, Glass Bottle, Pouch, Quiver, Sack, Vial, Waterskin | - | Full in 1d12 hours |

## Poison Food & Water
***Detecting if Food is Poison.*** Search action at the food source, Wisdom (Medicine) DC 18.
- *Success.* Can tell if the food source is poison or not.
- *Failure.* Cannot see any evidence of the food source being poison.

***Finding a Clean Spot to Collect Water.*** Search action at a water source, Wisdom (Survival) DC 16.
- *Success.* Find a safe spot to collect clean water from the water source. Chance of water being poison when collecting from that spot is lowered by half.
- *Failure.* Cannot tell where a safe spot to collect clean water from the water source is.

***Consuming Poison Food or Water.*** Creatures that have consumed poison food or water must make a DC 13 Constitution Saving Throw upon the morning of the next day (typically upon finishing a Long Rest).
- *Success.* No effect.
- *Failure.* The creature has the Poisoned condition. Each time the creature takes a Long Rest, they can make this Saving Throw again to end the Poisoned condition. If the creature consumed poison food or water again between Saving Throws to end the condition, they make the next Saving Throw with Disadvantage.

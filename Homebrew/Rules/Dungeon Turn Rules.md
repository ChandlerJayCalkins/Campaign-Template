# Dungeon Turn Rules
*Based on: [We Don't Play Dungeons Like They Used To](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zyl73XLJWhE)*

Dungeon Turns are a way to track players' turns and keep track of time in a dungeon. They can be used as an alternative to the standard time keeping of rounds that combat uses.

Dungeon Turns involve players taking an action on their turn, similar to they way they do in combat. A player's turn is called their Dungeon Turn, and the actions they can take are called Dungeon Actions. Dungeon Actions take approximately 10 minutes to complete. Each round of characters taking Dungeon Actions is called a Dungeon Round. Each Dungeon Round takes approximately 10 minutes to complete, after which players can take more Dungeon Actions. Initiative does not need to be rolled to determine a turn order for Dungeon Turns. Instead, the players can collaborate to choose who goes in which order on any given Dungeon Round. Dungeon Turns can sometimes be interrupted by encounters that require returning to standard time flow, such as combat, puzzles, or social encounters.

Below are examples of Dungeon Actions that creatures can take on their turn in a Dungeon Turn.

# Dungeon Actions

## Casting a Spell
***Prerequisite.*** *You pick a spell you can cast as a part of this Dungeon Action.*

You spend the next 10 minutes casting a spell that has a casting time of 10 minutes, or a ritual spell that has a casting time of 10 minutes and an action or less.

***Longer Casting Times.*** You can take this Dungeon Action multiple turns in a row to cast spells with a longer casting time than 10 minutes.

***Rolling Initiative.*** If you roll Initiative while taking this Dungeon Action, you can take the Magic Action on each of your turns to continue casting the spell.

## Helping Out
***Prerequisite.*** *You have a skill or tool proficiency relevant to the check being made.*

Chose a creature within your reach. You grant that creature advantage on ability checks made during this Dungeon Turn.

## Interacting
***Prerequisite.*** *None.*

You interact with an object or part of the environment for the next 10 minutes. This includes, but is not limited to:
- Reading notes, tablets, or instructions.
- Moving rubble, or clearing a path.
- Understanding an esoteric study, storage, or complicated device.

At the DM's discretion, these interactions may require one or more D20 Tests.

***Taking a Short Rest.*** If you have at least 1 Hit Point, you can have this time count towards taking a Short Rest. 60 consecutive minutes must be spent taking a Short Rest to gain benefits from it, and you cannot do anything more strenuous than reading, talking, eating, or standing watch during this time.

## Keeping Watch
***Prerequisite.*** *You're not Blinded or Deafened, and you can see your surroundings clearly.*

You stand watch, preparing for any event.

***Alert Your Allies.*** Any allies that can see or hear you cannot be surprised by hostile creatures you detect.

***Taking a Short Rest.*** If you have at least 1 Hit Point, you can have this time count towards taking a Short Rest. 60 consecutive minutes must be spent taking a Short Rest to gain benefits from it, and you cannot do anything more strenuous than reading, talking, eating, or standing watch during this time.

***Prepared Motion.*** If you roll Initiative, you can use a reaction before your first turn to take one of the following Actions:
- Attack (once)
- Dash
- Dodge
- Disengage
- Hide
- Magic Action to cast a Cantrip with a casting time of an Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction, or to use a magic item with a use time of an Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction

## Loot Everything
***Prerequisite.*** *You can see and hear clearly.*

You spend 10 minutes collecting nearby adventuring gear and valuables that have already been discovered or weren't hidden.

## Move Quietly
***Prerequisite.*** *Proficiency in Stealth.*

For the next 10 minutes, creatures must succeed on a Wisdom (Perception) ability check to find you. The DC to locate you is 8 + your Dexterity (Stealth) modifier.

## Search Area
***Prerequisite.*** *You can see and hear clearly.*

You spend 10 minutes searching a small room, or an area no larger than 50 feet in any dimension.

***Ability Check.*** Make a Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check (DM's choice).

*Success.* You find everything in this room hidden by a DC equal to or lower than the result of your check, such as traps, secret doors, concealed items, etc.

## Take Your Time
***Prerequisite.*** *None.*

You spend the next 10 minutes taking an Action.

***Taking 20.*** You get a 20 on your next D20 Test (before including modifiers). If that check would've had advantage, you instead get a +5 to the result.

***Replace Failure.*** When you take an Action to make an ability check and fail, you can instead spend 10 minutes doing it as this Dungeon Action.

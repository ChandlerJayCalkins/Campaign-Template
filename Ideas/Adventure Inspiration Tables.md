### Water

| d4  | Type of Water                                       |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Flowing water (river, stream, slide, etc.)          |
| 2   | Shallow puddles scattered around                    |
| 3   | Deep, still water (deep pond, lake, well, etc.)     |
| 4   | Vertical water (fountain, waterfall, geysers, etc.) |

### Manipulatable Terrain

| d4  | Type of Manipulatable Terrain                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Breakable (block passageways, give cover, break a piece off to use as a tool, etc.)                                                                                  |
| 2   | Moldable (turn into a tool / something useful, fool NPCs, create advantages like holes to shoot through, etc.)                                                       |
| 3   | Movable (block passageways, give cover, cause damage, etc.)                                                                                                          |
| 4   | Magic (thoughts change terrain, spellcasters can take magic action to change terrain, certain parts of terrain can be interacted with to change other terrain, etc.) |

### Terrain That Changes on its Own

| d4  | Type of Self Changing Terrain |
| --- | ----------------------------- |
| 1   | Goes up and down              |
| 2   | Blocks and unblocks passages  |
| 3   | Forces players to move        |
| 4   | Creates / removes cover       |

### Dangerous Terrain

| d6  | Types of Dangerous Terrain                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Traps                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 2   | Drops                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 3   | Dangerous to touch (very hot, acidic, poisonous, sharp, etc.)                                                                                                                                             |
| 4   | Magic effect (covers you in acid, turns you into a fish temporarily, freezes the ground with slippery ice, etc.)                                                                                          |
| 5   | Breakable (breaks to fall down / over, breaks beneath to create a drop, breaks to release something like water or a monster, etc.)                                                                        |
| 6   | Terrain that creates more danger (stepping on loud stone that wakes up monsters, stepping into a magic circle with fiends trapped inside, stepping on plants that anger surrounding flora into attacking) |

### Terrain That Doesn't Conform to Normal Expectations

| d4  | Type of Nonconforming Terrain                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Things should be solid that aren't solid, or vice versa (solid clouds, mushy stone, etc.)  |
| 2   | Things that aren't the temperature they should be (hot ice, cold fire, etc.)               |
| 3   | Terrain that moves that shouldn't be moving (stone with ocean-like waves, )                |
| 4   | Things that don't feel like they should (sharp carpet, sticky wood, vibrating stone, etc.) |

### Tactical Locations

| d6  | Type of Tactical Location                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | High point with cover (top of hill with bushes, top of a tower with walls, etc.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 2   | Secret passages between locations (tunnels between rooms, portals between sides of a field, etc.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3   | Cover (big rocks, pillars, foliage, hiding spot, etc.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 4   | Place that allows magic effects to be controlled (tree that changes weather, alter that summons dangerous creatures, rock that rolls around and touching it creates a globe of invulnerability, fire where throwing something into it causes gravity to flip / get stronger / get weaker at another location, etc.) |
| 5   | Vehicle (raft across water, wagon, sleds going downhill, etc.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 6   | Escape route (water slide, ladder, trapdoor, etc.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

# Puzzle Goals

| d10 | Puzzle Goal                                                                                  |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Escape a location before it's too late                                                       |
| 2   | Get past a door / lock / block into an area                                                  |
| 3   | Choose the correct items to craft / unlock something                                         |
| 4   | Change the environment (lift a curse, release a dam, open the ceiling to let light in, etc.) |
| 5   | Get past or disable a trap                                                                   |
| 6   | Try to sneak past something dangerous                                                        |
| 7   | Get a creature or magic phenomenon to help                                                   |
| 8   | Safely move an object or creature somewhere without damaging / hurting it                    |
| 9   | Obtain a status effect that would help with something else                                   |
| 10  | Destroy / dispose of an item correctly                                                       |

# Puzzle Mechanics

| d10 | Puzzle Mechanic                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Avoiding looking at something that hurts / impedes you, or looking at something to help you                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 2   | Light / shadows revealing hidden things                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 3   | Things doing the opposite of what you expect them to do (jumping over a pit takes you back to the side you started on while falling in takes you to the other side, turning a doorknob keeps a door magically sealed while locking it causes it to open, heating up ice causes it to freeze more while cooling it causes the ice to melt and reveal something inside, chopping down vines keeping someone trapped causes more to grow while watering them causes them to slither away, etc.) |
| 4   | Interacting with an alternate dimension / plane that allows you to do things you can't do in the prime material plane, like a dream world (from sleeping), the Ethereal Plane, the Feywild, an imaginary world made up by an NPC, etc.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 5   | Controlling an object to move to a goal by answering riddles or with a magic item, and if the wrong answer is given or the item is used wrong, then the object goes the wrong way into an obstacle                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 6   | Equipping / using the correct piece of equipment to complete a task                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 7   | Choosing between different creatures to fight, defeating the correct one gives you something you need while defeating the wrong one gives you nothing, or possibly even hinders you                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 8   | Certain prayers / songs / dances / arcane utterances under certain circumstances cause certain things to happen                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 9   | Moving the correct items / creatures around into the correct places at the correct times to cause certain things to happen                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 10  | A mechanism that unlocks various rooms, but only one room can be unlocked at a time and things that can't be moved from each room are needed elsewhere                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
